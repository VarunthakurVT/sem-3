## Median Practice and Ordering Data

Median calculation was practiced using small data sets discussed interactively with students

The key rule was stated: arrange the data first before finding the median

Several students gave different median answers, including 3, 4, and 5; the teacher checked the responses against the ordered data

The teacher confirmed that one student’s answer of 5 was correct for one example and that another answer of 3 was correct for a different example

The examples reinforced that the median depends on arranging the observations before selecting the middle value

## Mean and Standard Deviation Calculations

Mean calculation was introduced using a data set with a total of approximately 22

Students were asked to calculate the mean and then proceed to standard deviation

Standard deviation was presented through two formulas

Formula selection depends on the calculation result:

One formula can be used directly in the ordinary case

If the result appears in decimal or fractional form, the alternative formula may be more convenient

A value of 3.8 was mentioned as an example of a decimal result requiring the appropriate formula

Students were instructed to continue the calculation while the teacher monitored their work
