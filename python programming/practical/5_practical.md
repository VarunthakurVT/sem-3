## Overview

Education session assigning a destination-based task using three previously taught concepts

Participants: an instructor and students; individual names were not identified

Critical outcomes:

Students must create one destination using the three concepts

The problem involves an open jail, a prisoner, forest and “six” guards, and applying a NOT gate

Completing the practical task can earn a +5 bonus in the final practical exam

Students were told that solving the task may reasonably take up to two months

## Destination Task Using Three Concepts

Students must select or create one destination based on the three concepts covered in class

The class had already reached an intermediate stage of the problem involving:

An open jail

A prisoner

A forest guard

A “six” guard

Asking the same question to one person

The next logical step is to determine where a NOT gate should be applied in the solution

Successfully solving this stage leads toward the final practical-exam task

## Practical Examination Incentive and Timeline

Final practical examination: Completing the assigned problem provides a +5 bonus

Expected timeline: The instructor indicated that students could take “aaram se mahina,” understood in context as roughly two months, to solve it

Students confirmed their understanding and acceptance of the task
